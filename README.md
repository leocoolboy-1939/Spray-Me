## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Spray Me** is a versatile stencil-style font designed specifically for creatives who desire a spray-paint aesthetic in their designs. This **latest version** offers a unique dripping effect that can be toggled while typing, enhancing your creative process. Ideal for short-format designs like posters and apparel graphics, **Spray Me** is compatible with both Windows and macOS, ensuring accessibility for all users. With approximately 90 glyphs, including a dedicated spray-can dingbat and various decorative splatter glyphs, it provides flexibility without the need for external overlays. 

Download the **full version** today to elevate your design projects with a gritty, urban look that captures attention!
